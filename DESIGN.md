## Brand & Style
The design system establishes an editorial outdoor lifestyle aesthetic tailored for premium wilderness expeditions across the Indonesian archipelago—from the tropical alpine peaks of Carstensz Pyramid to the volcanic ridges of Mount Rinjani. It bridges high-altitude technical utility with quiet, artisanal luxury inspired by Snow Peak and Arc'teryx, grounded by authentic Indonesian outdoor heritage.

The target audience consists of experienced mountaineers, eco-conscious adventurers, and design-minded urban travelers seeking reliable, top-tier technical gear without the friction of outright ownership. The visual tone must evoke discipline, cinematic stillness, tactile durability, and reverence for nature.

### Design Movement: Tactical Minimalist Editorial
- **Composition:** Asymmetric editorial layouts balanced by strict structural grids, expansive warm negative space, and poster-like typography hierarchy.
- **Surface Quality:** Flat, tactile, organic layers. Strictly avoid synthetic glassmorphism, floating bubble containers, or over-rounded "toy-like" components.
- **Imagery Integration:** Full-bleed natural photography, desaturated volcanic landscapes, rich tropical moss tones, and high-detail gear close-ups framed by architectural hairline dividers.

## Colors
The palette balances dense equatorial canopy greens with sun-bleached volcanic tones and functional safety accents. The default experience is daylight editorial (`light` mode) anchored on tactile canvas surfaces rather than sterile digital whites.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#1B4332` - Forest Green):** The commanding tone for key structural elements, mastheads, primary action states, and high-impact editorial typography.
- **Secondary (`#2D6A4F` - Moss Green):** Supporting tone for secondary badges, subtle hover highlights, interactive state variations, and category headers.
- **Accent / Tertiary (`#D98B36` - Adventure Orange):** Reserved strictly for primary transactional triggers (e.g., "Reserve Dates"), live gear availability indicators, seasonal expedition callouts, and critical focal anchors.
- **Neutral Surface / Background (`#F5F1E8` - Warm Cream):** Canvas base for all main viewports, providing a warm, paper-like tactile feel that softens high-contrast gear photography.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#252525` - Deep Charcoal):** Primary body copy, iconography, and high-density technical specs. Avoid pure `#000000` to preserve the natural paper-print quality.
- **Structural Border & Sub-Surface (`#DAD7CD` - Sage Beige):** Muted hairline dividers, subtle card backings, disabled outlines, and structural layout borders.

## Typography
The typographic hierarchy leverages structural tension between vertical architectural condensed forms (Oswald) and neutral, highly-legible geometric modernism (Manrope).

### Typographic Roles
- **Headlines & Display (Oswald):** Set in deliberate uppercase or sentence-case for major expedition collection titles, editorial banners, and product model identifiers. Oswald gives catalog items an archival field-guide posture.
- **Body & Continuous Reading (Manrope):** Set in balanced weights (400 regular, 500 medium) to ensure effortless readability across gear technical specifications, sizing charts, and regional trail descriptions.
- **Micro-Labels & Technical Annotations (Manrope Caps):** Uppercase, letter-spaced labels provide military/technical equipment calibration aesthetics for status tags, specs (e.g., `4-SEASON`, `800-FILL`, `RIPSTOP NYLON`), and rental intervals.
- **Rental Pricing (Oswald Numeric):** High-clarity numerical hierarchy allows immediate scanning of daily and multi-day rates (e.g., `Rp 185.000 / day`).

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows an authoritative 12-column editorial grid on desktop, scaling to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. It relies on architectural discipline: alignment lines run uninterrupted, and negative space is deliberately preserved to evoke rugged, open landscapes.

### Responsive Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns. Gutter: `1.5rem`. Margin: `1.25rem`. Complex technical comparisons collapse into horizontal kinetic carousels or stacked accordions.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8 columns. Gutter: `1.5rem`. Margin: `2.5rem`. Catalog items sit in 2-column or 3-column asymmetric configurations.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns. Gutter: `2rem`. Margin: `4rem`. Max container bound: `1440px`. Accommodates deep multi-filter technical navigation alongside spacious 3-to-4 column product cards.

### Spacing Usage Rules
- `space-xs` (4px): Micro gaps between label icons and technical indicators.
- `space-sm` (8px): Spacing inside compact pill badges and between form field labels and inputs.
- `space-md` (16px): Internal cell padding for catalog cards, field inputs, and navigation links.
- `space-lg` (24px): Gap between catalog card details and price modules; standard content group spacing.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section transitions within product detail templates and editorial feature offsets.

## Elevation & Depth
In line with tactile outdoor editorial philosophy, the system rejects glossy floatation, heavy blur drop-shadows, and artificial skeuomorphism. Visual depth is articulated through **tonal layering and crisp structural hairline rules**.

### Depth Layers
- **Ground Floor (Base Canvas):** Set to Warm Cream (`#F5F1E8`). Represents the foundational field paper.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card & Module Canvas):** Clean, crisp surfaces tinted in subtle off-cream (`#FFFFFF` with `#F5F1E8` bleed) or bordered with Sage Beige (`#DAD7CD`, 1px solid).
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Technical Drawers & Menus):** Pure white canvas overlay enclosed in a defining 1px Forest Green (`#1B4332`) hairline border.

### Shadows (Restricted & Directional)
- When depth is required for popovers or sticky reservation bars, use an ambient, low-spread architectural offset: `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(37, 37, 37, 0.06);`.
- Avoid colored glowing shadows around buttons. Interactive depth is conveyed via border-weight transitions or direct surface color shifts.

## Shapes
The shape philosophy favors crisp, utilitarian geometry that mirrors technical expedition crates, topo maps, and structural pack equipment. Curvature is kept strictly minimal (`roundedness: 1` — Soft, 4px baseline).

### Corner Treatment
- **Standard UI Containers (Cards, Modals, Inputs):** 4px radius (`rounded-sm`). Delivers a refined, professional finish without feeling harsh or rounded.
- **Buttons & Tactical Tags:** 2px to 4px radius. Absolutely avoid circular capsule/pill shapes for general controls to maintain an intentional, utilitarian silhouette.
- **Media Containers:** Sharp or 4px subtle curve with an inner 1px Sage Beige border stroke to treat photographs like archival plates.

## Components

### 1. Catalog Product Cards
- **Structure:** Framed in a 1px solid `#DAD7CD` border over a `#FFFFFF` base with 4px border radius.
- **Image Frame:** 4:5 aspect ratio photograph set against a muted volcanic stone or studio warm gray background.
- **Header:** Top-aligned technical tags (e.g., `TENT // 4-PERSON`) rendered in Manrope Caps (`11px`, letter-spaced) in `#2D6A4F`.
- **Title:** Oswald Medium (`20px`) in Charcoal (`#252525`).
- **Footer Module:** High-contrast bottom bar separating specs from pricing:
  - Daily Rate: Oswald Bold (`22px`) in `#1B4332` + `/ day` in Manrope (`12px`, `#252525`).
  - Quick-Reserve Action: Low-profile secondary icon or text button in `#D98B36`.

### 2. Buttons & Actions
- **Primary CTA ("Book Expedition / Rent"):** Solid Adventure Orange (`#D98B36`) with `#FFFFFF` or Deep Charcoal (`#252525`) text, Oswald semi-bold caps (`14px`), 4px border radius. Hover state: `#C27725`.
- **Secondary Action ("View Specs"):** Ghost button with 1.5px Forest Green (`#1B4332`) border and matching text. Hover state: Forest Green fill with Warm Cream text.
- **Tertiary / Filter Triggers:** Warm Cream background with Sage Beige (`#DAD7CD`) 1px border, transitioning to Forest Green text on hover.

### 3. Date & Duration Range Pickers
- **Visuals:** Grid-based calendar mimicking mountaineering route logs.
- **Selected Expedition Span:** Filled with a continuous Moss Green (`#2D6A4F`) solid band; range start and end markers highlighted with an Adventure Orange (`#D98B36`) border pip.

### 4. Input Fields & Selectors
- **Base Style:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid `#DAD7CD`, 4px radius, `12px 16px` padding.
- **Focus State:** 1.5px border in Forest Green (`#1B4332`) with zero generic browser glow.
- **Labels:** Positioned above inputs in Manrope Bold Caps (`11px`), colored `#2D6A4F`.

### 5. Chips, Status Tags & Weather Badges
- **Availability Badges:** Compact rectangular badges with 2px radius:
  - Available: Pale Moss tint background with `#1B4332` text.
  - Reserved / Maintenance: Subdued charcoal tint with `#252525` text.
- **Expedition Grade Tags:** Hairline-bordered `#DAD7CD` badges indicating terrain certification (e.g., `SUMMIT GRADE`, `ALPINE PROOF`, `RAIN SHIELD`).

### 6. Technical Specifications Table
- Compact data grid rendered with alternating Warm Cream (`#F5F1E8`) and White (`#FFFFFF`) striping.
- Split-column layout: Label in Manrope Medium (`13px`, `#2D6A4F`), Value in Manrope Semi-Bold (`13px`, `#252525`) with a subtle 1px dashed Sage Beige border separator.
